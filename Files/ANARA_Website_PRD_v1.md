## 1. Company Overview

### 1.1 ANARA

**ANARA** stands for:

> **A New Approach to Real-world Advancement**

ANARA Technologies is a technology company focused on building practical digital systems that address real-world needs.

The company operates in two primary areas:

#### ANARA Systems

Products and systems conceptualized and developed under ANARA Technologies to address specific real-world challenges.

The first system under ANARA is **PLANTWAIS**.

#### Custom Software Development

ANARA works with businesses and organizations to understand their problems, processes, and needs, then develops appropriate digital systems around them.

## 5. Market Positioning

### Primary Positioning

> **Digital Systems for Growing Businesses**

ANARA will initially focus on small and growing businesses, entrepreneurs, startups, and organizations that need practical digital solutions.

ANARA will not position itself as a company that builds every possible type of software. Instead, the company will focus on understanding specific problems and determining how technology can reasonably help solve them.

### Key Message

> **Tell us what’s slowing your business down. We’ll explore how technology can help.**

---

## 6. Target Market

Initial target clients include:

- Small and growing businesses / MSMEs
- Entrepreneurs
- Startups
- Small organizations and associations
- Cooperatives
- Organizations transitioning from manual processes to digital systems

Typical problems may include:

- Manual record keeping
- Spreadsheet-heavy workflows
- Repetitive administrative work
- Disorganized information
- Inefficient business processes
- Difficulty managing day-to-day operations

The initial market does not permanently limit ANARA. The company may serve larger organizations and undertake more complex projects as its capabilities, portfolio, resources, and team grow.

## 10. Brand Personality

ANARA should feel:

- **Professional but approachable** — trustworthy without sounding overly corporate.
- **Modern but simple** — contemporary without unnecessary futuristic styling.
- **Clear** — language ordinary business owners can understand.
- **Grounded** — focused on real problems and practical solutions.
- **Forward-looking** — ambitious while remaining realistic.

---

## 11. Communication Style

Website content should use clear and understandable language.

Avoid unnecessary technical jargon and generic corporate statements such as:

> “Leveraging cutting-edge technologies to drive digital transformation.”

Prefer direct language such as:

> **Tell us what’s slowing your business down. We’ll explore how technology can help.**

The website should communicate primarily with business owners and organizations rather than software developers.

---

## 12. Visual Identity

### Primary Brand Gradient

- **Deep Magenta:** `#670022`
- **ANARA Magenta:** `#B0004B`
- **Bright Magenta:** `#EB347C`

Primary gradient:

```text
#670022 → #B0004B → #EB347C
```

### Supporting Colors

- White
- Near-black
- Neutral grays

### Website Theme

The website should be **primarily light with strategic dark sections**.

Magenta should act as the distinctive ANARA accent rather than dominate every section.

### Design Characteristics

- Clean
- Spacious
- Modern
- Strong typography
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Subtle animations
- Responsive
- Product-focused
- Rounded elements where appropriate

### Avoid

- Generic corporate blue
- Excessive gradients
- Excessive animation
- Generic futuristic AI graphics
- Unnecessary stock photography
- Overly complicated layouts
- Cliché images of people pointing at laptops

Use actual product/interface visuals where possible.

### Logo

The official ANARA logo asset is available at `frontend/src/assets/anara-logo.png`.

- Use it as the site logo in the header and footer.
- Import it through the Vite asset pipeline and preserve its proportions.
- Do not stretch, distort, recolor, redraw, regenerate, or otherwise modify the logo file.
- Provide accessible alt text.
- On dark surfaces, adapt the surrounding presentation (for example a light chip) rather than modifying the logo.

# 17. PLANTWAIS Product Page

The PLANTWAIS page should function as a product showcase.

Required sections:

1. Hero
2. The Problem
3. The Solution
4. What PLANTWAIS Can Do
5. Who It’s For
6. How It Works
7. Product Preview
8. Development Status
9. About the Project
10. Contact CTA

### The Problem

> Farming takes investment, but it can be difficult to know what to expect before you start. How much will you need? What will you spend on? How much could you earn? Without a clear plan, farmers may have to make these decisions along the way.

### The Solution

Memorable message:

> **Plan before you plant.**

> PLANTWAIS helps farmers plan before they start. It creates a farm enterprise plan that shows the expected activities, costs, and potential returns before a production cycle begins — giving farmers a clearer picture of what it may take to start and what they could expect from their farm business.

The memorable message suits planting enterprises; keep the supporting copy inclusive of production cycles that do not literally involve planting (for example, broiler or milkfish).

### Support & Recognition

PLANTWAIS received support through the Department of Agriculture's Young Farmers Challenge Program.

Approved wording:

> **Supported through the Department of Agriculture's Young Farmers Challenge Program**

Accuracy rules: do not state or imply official DA endorsement, partnership, or ownership, or that PLANTWAIS is a DA system. Do not mention grant amounts. Keep this recognition visually secondary to the ANARA and PLANTWAIS brands.

### Development Status

The product must be represented transparently as **In Development**.

A future milestone view may use:

> **Research → Development → Validation → Pilot → Launch**

### Content Boundaries

The public page should focus on explaining PLANTWAIS to users, partners, and organizations.

Do not publicly expose detailed internal:

- Technical architecture
- Database schema
- Internal implementation roadmap
- Private development documentation

unless explicitly approved later.

### CTA

> **Interested in PLANTWAIS?**

CTA:

**Contact ANARA**

Do not use `Get Started` while the product is not publicly launched.

# 22. Project Inquiry Workflow

Client journey:

> **Website → Start a Project → Submit Inquiry → ANARA Review → Discovery Conversation → Scope → Proposal → Agreement → Development → Deployment → Support**

Submitting an inquiry does **not** mean ANARA has accepted the project.

After submission:

> **Thanks for telling us about your project.**

> ANARA will review your inquiry and contact you to better understand your needs and discuss whether we can help.

The proposal process after discovery should generally follow:

> **Problem → Proposed Solution → Scope → Features → Timeline → Cost**

# 24. Technical Direction

## Frontend

**React + Vite**

## Styling

**Tailwind CSS**

## Backend

**Node.js + Express**, only where server-side functionality is actually required.

Do not add backend infrastructure merely to make the architecture more complex.

## Database

No database is required initially unless a concrete V1 requirement needs persistent storage.

## Forms

Contact and project inquiries should be securely delivered to ANARA's official business email through an appropriate server-side or transactional email solution.

## Hosting

Initial frontend hosting may use **Vercel**.

Backend hosting should be determined only if server-side functionality is required.

## Domain

ANARA should use a dedicated company domain. Final domain selection depends on availability.

# 25. Non-Functional Requirements

## Performance

- Fast page loads
- Optimized images
- Lazy loading where appropriate
- Avoid unnecessary large assets
- Avoid excessive JavaScript

## Security

- No secrets or private API keys exposed in frontend code
- CAPTCHA protection
- Input validation
- Secure form processing
- Basic abuse prevention

## Accessibility

- Appropriate color contrast
- Readable typography
- Keyboard navigation
- Proper form labels
- Alt text for meaningful images
- Semantic HTML
- Visible focus states

## Responsiveness

All core functionality must remain usable across supported screen sizes.

## Browser Support

Support current versions of:

- Google Chrome
- Microsoft Edge
- Mozilla Firefox
- Safari

## Reliability

Forms must provide clear:

- Loading state
- Success state
- Error state

## Maintainability

Use reusable components and organized content structures that support future expansion.

## Quality Target

Aim for strong production Lighthouse results, ideally **90+** in:

- Performance
- Accessibility
- Best Practices
- SEO

This is a quality target rather than a reason to distort the product or hide legitimate functionality.
